Atlas Tracker Privacy Policy

Updated November 19, 2026.

Atlas Tracker stores tracking records locally. The free TestFlight build 5 includes experimental Atlas AI. The record-keeping edition prepared for App Store review omits the chat and its model. Atlas does not operate an account, cloud synchronization, advertising, or analytics service for these records. Exports, device backups, support messages and Apple platform services have separate data flows described below.

Records stored on your device

The app stores compound names, schedules, dose amounts and times, injection sites, reported effects, notes, weights, inventory and preferences in its app container. Versions with journaling may also store journal entries, goals and attachments you select. It uses Core Data for tracking records and system preferences for settings. In builds with AI chat, recent chat history is saved in a local file. These records support logging, reminders, reference lookup and local charts.

Atlas AI

In builds that include it, Atlas AI is designed to generate educational responses on-device using bundled llama/ggml runtime code, a Qwen-derived model and a MiniLM reference encoder. Its normal release path does not send prompts, retrieved context or answers to a remote model provider. The app includes third-party notices and an artifact manifest. Local AI can be inaccurate; it does not provide medical care, diagnosis or treatment.

Chat storage retains a bounded recent history. Older messages are removed when history is read or saved according to the app retention window; this is not a promise of background deletion at an exact clock time. You can clear chat history in the app. The current chat directory is marked to be excluded from device backups; previously created backups or exported copies are not retroactively deleted. Developer-only simulator configurations are separate from distributed on-device builds.

Optional Apple Health import

The current Settings import asks Apple Health for permission to read body-weight entries and copies selected entries into Atlas. It does not automatically write Atlas records to Apple Health or request other Health data types. You can revoke Health access in Apple's Health permission controls. Revoking access does not delete records already imported into Atlas; remove those records separately if desired. Apple controls the Health store and any Health synchronization enabled in your Apple settings.

File protection and app lock

Atlas requests Apple's complete file protection for its Core Data store files and current chat-history file. This uses iOS data protection; Atlas does not add a separate SQLCipher database or an app-managed database encryption key. Protection varies with the file, device state and system configuration, and is not an assurance that every export, preference, backup or previously saved copy has identical protection.

If enabled, Face ID or Touch ID uses Apple's authentication service. Atlas receives the authentication result, not your face or fingerprint data. Keep your device passcode and system settings secure.

Exports, backups and external links

CSV dose-history files and PDF reports are created locally when you request them. Full reports can include dose details, reported effects, notes, weights and journal records for the selected dates. In versions offering Dose Summary Only, that choice limits the file to recorded dates and times, compound names, amounts, units and record types. The report formats are not complete app backups and cannot restore all relationships and settings. Exported report content is readable by the destination you select; it is not protected by a separate Atlas password. Sharing a report or saving it to a cloud-backed location sends that copy to the service you choose.

Atlas does not run a cloud sync service. In the free TestFlight build, iOS or computer backups can include eligible tracking records according to your device settings. The chat directory requests backup exclusion. The prepared App Store edition also asks iOS to exclude the tracking database and journal attachments from automatic device backups. Apple ultimately controls system backups. Use Back Up Tracking Data to keep a copy you can restore before changing phones or deleting Atlas. App deletion or Reset All Data does not erase external backups, Apple Health records, emailed material or files already shared elsewhere. See Apple's documentation for your device backup settings.

Opening support, policy or source links uses the selected email app or browser, which can contact external services under their own policies. These actions are initiated by you.

Support and beta feedback

If you email support or send price feedback, we receive the email address and content you choose to send. We use them to answer your request or evaluate the proposed price. Please use fictional examples or redact health records before sending screenshots.

When you request a paid-launch notice using our optional Google Form, Google stores the email address you enter and the time of submission for AtlasTracker. Only enter your email address. We use it for one email when the paid iPhone app launches. You can also request or cancel a notice or ask us to delete a response by emailing krisco65@gmail.com. We remove the launch list after sending the notice. Our email provider and Google's standard backup retention may retain copies for a limited period.

App Store and TestFlight distribution, system diagnostics and feedback sent through Apple's tools are handled by Apple and may make diagnostic or feedback information available to the developer. Atlas does not embed an advertising, analytics or remote crash-reporting SDK. This distinction does not mean Apple or an email provider collects nothing.

Your controls

You can edit or delete records, clear chat history, change notification and biometric preferences, and choose whether to import or share data. Reset All Data removes the app's tracking records, custom compounds, chat and settings, then restores the default reference library. External copies remain under the control of their storage provider or recipient.

Atlas is intended for adults. Its App Store age rating is determined by Apple's current questionnaire and regional rules; it is not a medical suitability assessment.

Contact and policy changes

Contact: krisco65@gmail.com. Use this address for support, privacy questions or requests about correspondence you have sent us. We do not sell tracking records or use them for advertising. This policy will be updated when app behavior changes; the date above identifies this revision.
