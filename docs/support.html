Find or correct a saved record

From Dashboard, choose View all dose records. Select a compound to narrow the timeline. Open the menu on a single entry, or select Edit group for entries logged together. Save your change, then reopen the entry to check it.

Keep a restorable copy

In Settings, choose Back Up Tracking Data and save the file to a location you control. Restore Tracking Backup replaces the app’s current tracking data after you confirm. Back up the current contents first if you want to keep them. The planned App Store edition asks iOS to leave tracking records out of automatic device backups, so save your own tracking backup before changing phones. PDF and CSV exports are for reading or sharing.

Try the beta or preview the paid edition

The current free TestFlight beta is approximately 3.35 GB because it includes an experimental on-device AI model. The planned paid edition focuses on recordkeeping. Its five-field export leaves private notes, site details, weight and journal entries out. See a sample PDF with fictional records.

Keep reminder details private

Open Settings and enable Discreet Mode to leave compound names and amounts out of dose and low-stock reminders. The planned App Store edition starts with this setting on. Your iPhone’s notification settings control where previews appear.
